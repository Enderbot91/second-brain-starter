# Soul interview prompt

Paste this into any AI chat, answer the questions in plain language, and have it fill `soul.template.md`. Twenty minutes. The answers matter more than the template.

---

You are helping me write a one-page operator profile called `soul.md`. Every AI tool I use will read it first, every session, so it has to be short, concrete, and in my own voice. Ask me the twelve questions below one at a time. Wait for each answer. Push back once if an answer is vague ("what does that look like on a Tuesday?"). Do not move on until you could write the section from what I said. When all twelve are answered, fill the template I give you next, keep it under 600 words, and show me the draft. Hard rules for the draft: no em dashes anywhere, no "it's not X, it's Y" sentences, no corporate filler.

1. What are the two or three roles you switch between in a normal week? Name each one and say who the key people are in it.
2. In each role, what is the thing that costs real money or carries a legal penalty if you miss it? Name the actual filings, payments, or deadlines.
3. When two things compete for your attention, what wins? Walk me through a real conflict from the last month and what you chose.
4. How far ahead do you want to be warned about a deadline, and in what form (a line in a digest, a ping, an email)?
5. What do assistants and tools get wrong about you most often? What have you had to correct more than once?
6. How technical are you? What do you want explained and what do you want skipped?
7. Show me two short messages you wrote recently that sound like you. One casual, one to someone you report to.
8. How do you open and sign a message? Any words or patterns you never use?
9. Where do your files live, and what devices do you work from? Where are secrets kept?
10. Who are the five people whose names will come up most, and what is each one's role relative to you?
11. What is the one recurring personal commitment that work is not allowed to eat?
12. Finish this sentence as bluntly as you can: "The simplest thing that would actually help me is..."

After the draft, ask me to read it out loud and strike anything I would not say.

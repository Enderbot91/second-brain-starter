# Glossary and IDs

Quick reference for the acronyms and numbers that come up constantly at Redbud Water. Agents read this instead of guessing. Every ID below is a placeholder for the demo.

## Regulators and programs

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| TCEQ | Texas Commission on Environmental Quality. State drinking water regulator. |
| SDWA | Safe Drinking Water Act. The federal law TCEQ enforces. |
| PWS ID | Public Water System identification number. Ours is TX0000000 (placeholder). |
| CCR | Consumer Confidence Report. Annual water quality report to customers, due July 1 each year, certification to TCEQ after delivery. |
| MCL | Maximum Contaminant Level. The legal limit for a regulated contaminant. |
| TTHM | Total Trihalomethanes. Disinfection byproduct, sampled quarterly, 80 ppb MCL as a running annual average. |
| HAA5 | Haloacetic acids. Sampled with TTHM. 60 ppb MCL. |
| BacT | Monthly bacteriological (coliform) samples. Two per month for our size. |
| USDA RD | USDA Rural Development. Our loan and grant lender for the Well 3 and tank work. |
| LOC | Letter of Conditions. The USDA RD document that lists everything we must satisfy before closing. |
| WSC | Water Supply Corporation. Our nonprofit member-owned entity type. |
